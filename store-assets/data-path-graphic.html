How your meetings get here

The TranscriptExporter extension checks for new Granola, Fathom, or Fireflies meetings every 10 minutes and hands each one to this plugin over 127.0.0.1.

No cloud. No account. Nothing leaves your computer. Notes you already have are never touched.
